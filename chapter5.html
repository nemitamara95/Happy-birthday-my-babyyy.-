CHAPTER FIVE

A Letter For You, My Love

There's something here for you...

Oh my baby, Michael,

I don't know what to say or where to start.

The last month has been one of the hardest months for us since we've been together. More than once, the thought that I might be losing you crossed my mind. And you would know, because every time, you kept reassuring me and reminding me just how much you love me.

I know I'm not always the easiest person to please. I have my not-so-pleasing traits, and I know I can be a lot sometimes. But somehow, you make it work. You make me want to be better, and I love you even more for that.

You are one of the brightest people I know. You always try to find the positive in every situation. You're exceptional — smart, kind, hardworking, disciplined, my typeee, and so much more.

I still remember the night we started talking. You listened to me rant about an issue I was having with a male friend. I remember telling you how stupid I felt, and you said, and I quote:

"Fall in love with me, you won't feel stupid."

And if I said I haven't felt stupid in the last 1 year, 3 months and 13 days we've been together, I'd be lying. 😂

I've felt stupid. I've felt angry. I've felt confused. I've felt a lot of things — many of them probably because I still have so much to learn about communicating properly.

But through all of that, you have remained gentle and calm with me. You've continued to make me feel better about myself, even when I wasn't doing the best job of doing that myself.

You are my escape from an unfair world.

And I never want to lose that.

I hope and pray that, as you reach this milestone of your 20th year on earth, things begin to work out in your favour. I hope you find the answers you're looking for, figure things out, and become everything you're meant to be.

May God bless you, my love.

Live long and thrive. ❤️

I can't express how much I love you with words alone. I just hope my actions manage to show you, even in the little things, how much you mean to me.

I wish you the very best in life. I hope we continue to celebrate more birthdays, more years, more growth, more memories, and more life together.

Happy 20th birthday, my babyyy.

I love you more than I know how to put into words.

Forever yours,

Tam Tam ❤️
